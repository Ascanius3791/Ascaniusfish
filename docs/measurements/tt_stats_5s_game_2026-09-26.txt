a slot at this depth (an entry deepened in place by iterative deepening keeps its first depth).
lost->asked: discarded entries of this depth requested again later (%lost: share of all discarded).
deep-*: the lost entry was at least as deep as the request (exact: could have returned; bound: could
have tightened alpha/beta at equal depth). shallow: it would only have ordered moves.
reuse dist: insertions between the discard and the first request; far above capacity means the
table is too small, far below it means the replacement policy threw out something needed soon.
