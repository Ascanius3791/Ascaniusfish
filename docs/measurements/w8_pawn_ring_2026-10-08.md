# Weight set 8: pawns on the king ring (#102)

`issue/102` at 8454ca3 (main 5509a07 + the pawn attackers), 2026-10-08.

## The term

Every king-ring square an enemy pawn attacks adds one attacker to the king's count, as
`kingAttackersCount` does in classical Stockfish: it opens the `ks_min_attackers` gate and
multiplies the pieces' summed `ks_attacker_weight`, pawns add no weight or hits of their own.
Squares two of the king's own pawns defend don't count (Stockfish trims them from the whole
ring; here only from the pawn count), so a pawn on h4 hitting g3 in front of f2/g2/h2 is no
attacker.

How often, on `data/tune2/valid.tsv` (304770 positions, 609540 kings; a scratch probe before
the trim): an enemy pawn hits the ring of 33.8% of kings. The gate (attackers >= 2) is open
for 23.7% of kings without the term; counting squares it opens for another 19.9%, 3.9% with
no piece attacking at all.
